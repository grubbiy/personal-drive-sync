Personal Drive Sync

Keep Google Drive files available in a local Documents folder.

This is Vegard Laland's personal configuration of rclone. It is intended to synchronize the owner's Google Drive with the Documents folder on the owner's Linux computer.

What it does

After the owner authorizes access through Google, rclone can list, download, upload, update and delete Drive files to keep both locations in sync. Local copies of ordinary files are available offline. Native Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are represented by links and opened through Google.

This is a personal setup, not a hosted file-sync service. This website only provides information about it and does not sign visitors in or receive their Drive files.
